# Brief style guide

- **Lead with the answer.** The first line of the summary answers the question directly.
- **Plain English.** Short sentences, everyday words, no marketing language. Explain any acronym
  the first time it appears.
- **Numbers carry units and dates.** "£4,500 in 2024", not "about four and a half thousand".
- **Separate fact from judgement.** Facts get a source marker like `[1]`; your own assessment says
  so ("On balance, ...").
- **Confidence** is one of: *High* (several independent, recent sources agree), *Medium* (one good
  source, or sources partly disagree), *Low* (thin, old or conflicting evidence, or no sources
  available). Say why in one sentence.
- **Open questions** are things that would change the answer if known. At most three.
- **Sources** are numbered in the order first cited: title, publisher or site, date, and URL when
  there is one. Files from the workspace are cited by path.
- Length: aim for 300–600 words. Cut before you add.
